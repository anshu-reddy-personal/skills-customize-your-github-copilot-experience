📊 Ranking Methodology

Rankings are determined by a weighted composite score across five factors. Raw data comes from the National Park Service, IUCN species records, and visitor survey data updated annually. Each factor is scored 1–10 and multiplied by its weight to produce a final composite score.

Data sources: NPS Stats (irma.nps.gov), IUCN Red List, NPS biodiversity databases, and editorial review. Rankings are subjective and intended as a starting point for exploration rather than a definitive list. See README.md for full methodology documentation.
